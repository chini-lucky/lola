The blueprint for building the app. Every screen uses the same sample: Oct pay period, 28 Sep – 27 Oct 2026. Salary 20,000, overtime 15 h × 150 = 2,250, total 22,250, paid 0, balance 22,250. Leave Mon 19 Oct, holidays 13 and 23 Oct. Today is Fri 16 Oct.

Everyone

Login, sign-up and the waiting screen work for anyone. New accounts stay locked until an admin approves them. Account lives behind the person icon (Lola) or in Settings (admin).

Lola (nanny)

Read only. One action: confirm the period. Payslip and receipts can be downloaded for any period.

Admin

Same period view with edit controls, plus sheets for days and payments, and Settings with People and the change log.

Payslip PDF

Generated in the browser, one per pay period. A DRAFT stamp shows while the period is not over or not fully paid.
